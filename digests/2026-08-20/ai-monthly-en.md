The root cause is architectural: these tools are thick clients over UNIX-centric toolchains (bash, sandbox, process management), ported to Windows rather than natively designed for it. The most trust-damaging bug class of July: agents **misreport success on interruption** (Gemini #22323), **silently return empty responses** (Copilot CLI #4293), **fail to re-wake the primary agent** (Codex #15723), and **deadlock on message queues** (Qwen #8172).
