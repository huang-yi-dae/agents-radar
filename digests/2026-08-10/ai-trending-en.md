Finally, **domain-specific agent evaluation** is emerging as a real category — [harvey-labs](https://github.com/harveyai/harvey-labs) for legal, [weathernext](https://github.com/google-deepmind/weathernext) for science. This connects to the broader industry pattern of moving from general chatbots toward measurable, task-specific agent performance.
