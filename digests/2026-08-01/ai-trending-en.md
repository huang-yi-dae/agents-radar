## 3. Trend Signal Analysis

Today's trending data shows that **"agent skills" and "harnesses" are the new explosive layer**, above models and frameworks. [reverse-skill](https://github.com/zhaoxuya520/reverse-skill), [last30days-skill](https://github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill), and [ECC](https://github.com/affaan-m/ECC) all treat skills as portable, routable modules across Claude Code, Cursor, Cline, Codex, and other coding agents. The rapid rise of [openwork](https://github.com/different-ai/openwork) (+806 today) as an open-source alternative to Claude Cowork suggests a strong community desire to own the "agent coworking" layer rather than rent it. The [GitHub Copilot SDK](https://github.com/github/copilot-sdk) reinforces this: agentic coding is becoming a platform surface.

A distinct new direction is **post-vector RAG**. [PageIndex](https://github.com/VectifyAI/PageIndex) calls itself "vectorless, reasoning-based RAG," [Graphify](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify) builds knowledge graphs without a vector store, and [LEANN](https://github.com/StarTrail-org/LEANN) claims 97% storage savings for personal on-device RAG. Token-efficiency projects like [headroom](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom) and [Caveman](https://github.com/JuliusBrussee/caveman) also point to a cost-optimization mindset as agents run longer and consume more context.

Finally, the model landscape is shifting quickly: [Ollama](https://github.com/ollama/ollama) now supports Kimi-K2.6, GLM-5.2, MiniMax, DeepSeek, gpt-oss, Qwen, and Gemma, while educational projects like [tiny-llm](https://github.com/skyzh/tiny-llm) and [toyllm](https://github.com/ai-glimpse/toyllm) teach builders to create inference stacks from scratch. The combination of open-weight releases, local runtime improvements, and memory-rich agent harnesses is pushing the ecosystem toward **self-hosted, skill-driven, and context-efficient AI workflows**.
